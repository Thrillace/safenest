About SafeNest Metals Vault

Your trusted partner in premium asset protection since 2010

Secure. Private. Impenetrable.

Founded in London in 2010, SafeNest Metals Vault has established itself as the premier provider of high-security storage solutions in the United Kingdom. Our facility combines cutting-edge technology with traditional security measures to offer unparalleled protection for your valuables.

Located in the heart of London's financial district, our vault features biometric access controls, 24/7 armed security, and redundant systems to ensure your assets are always safe.

Military-Grade Security

Multi-layered protection with biometric scanners and motion detectors

Discrete Service

Complete privacy with anonymous storage options available

Fully Insured

All assets covered by Lloyd's of London insurance policies
